Pages and chapters

Each chapter starts at a heading of its own. The builder numbers the chapters and links each page to the pages beside it.

Chapter 2

Quoting files

An include quotes a file as it stands when the book is built. A quote holds no fence of its own.

An excerpt quotes the lines from one anchor to another.

A fence of the book's own kind goes through its hook:

A greeting: HELLO, READER

A plain fence is code, escaped as the renderer escapes it:
