Safari Walk

A ride down a dirt road across a dry savanna at golden hour, as the sun sets ahead of you: a new take on Safari Ride, the canvas game on this site, in 3D.

Its logic is written in Roc, on Rocco, Braeden Foster's 3D engine for Roc games, from Steve's fork with changes for Firefox. It needs a browser with WebGPU. AI agents (Claude) wrote the code, under Steve Howell's supervision.

Space pauses and resumes. F shows a frame readout. Every 20 seconds of walking, a frame report goes to the browser's console, ready to copy.
