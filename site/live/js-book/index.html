JavaScript in Roc: A Compiler and an Interpreter

A small book about roc-js-interpreter: a compiler of a subset of JavaScript to x86-64 and a bytecode VM for the same subset, both written in Roc, and what building them taught about Roc's values, its two backends and its build times.
