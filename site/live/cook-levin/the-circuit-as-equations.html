Chapter 16

The circuit as equations

“The circuit, in full” draws the decider as boxes. This chapter writes every gate of it as one Boolean equation, 1,439 in all, as “The polynomials, in full” prints every term. The names are those of “The machine as a circuit”, led by the core and the step: core2.step3.hb' is core 2's high bit of AX after line 3. The 16 inputs come first, then each core's input as two constants, core2.in_hb = 1, and the last line is decides.

python/equations.py reads this text back with a parser and an interpreter of its own, apart from the C that printed it, and evaluates it on all 4,096 programs and all 16 want registers at once: decides is 1 at exactly the 4,096 points where the program decides the problem the register holds. build.shale runs it on every build.
