Chapter 14

The circuit, in full

“The machine as a circuit” counts a core at 294 gates; this chapter draws every one of them, as “The polynomials, in full” prints every term. It is one core: the input's two bits, in_hb and in_lb, and the program's twelve, op1_hb to op6_lb, come in, and accepted comes out. Each box is one stepper of 49 gates, and the flags start from the constant 0.

The drawing is circuit_main core's output, drawn by Graphviz, and the book's build holds the source below it to that output, byte for byte. It keeps its own size; the box scrolls both ways.
