Appendix: two DRAT proofs

Here are the raw files behind two rows of the table in “The solver's work, in full”: rocsat's DRAT proofs, printed lemma by lemma, for anyone who wants to watch a solver rule out every program of a given length.

Each is a file of sat/proofs/, checked again by src/drat.c at every build of this book, as “The solver's work, in full” tells.
